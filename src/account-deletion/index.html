Delete Your Allos Account

This page lets you request deletion of your Allos account without the app. Allos is published on Google Play and the App Store by Allos Development.

Deleting your account is permanent. Your account and associated data — profile, completed-session history, settings tied to your account, and your customer records with our subscription-management and email providers — will be deleted, except where retention is legally required.

The fastest way to delete your account is in the app, under Settings → Account → Delete Account.

Deleting your Allos account does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription. Store billing continues until you cancel the subscription in your store account — manage subscriptions with Apple or Google Play. Cancelling is not required to delete your account.

To request deletion here, email hello@allos.app from the email address on your Allos account, with the subject “Account deletion request.” We’ll reply to verify that you control the address — nothing is deleted until then — and confirm once deletion is complete, typically within a few days and always within 30 days. If you no longer have access to that address, use the in-app deletion flow above — it verifies your identity by signing in again, not by email.
