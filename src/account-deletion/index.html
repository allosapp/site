Delete Your Allos Account

This page lets you request deletion of your Allos account without the app. Allos is published on Google Play and the App Store by Allos Development.

Deleting your account is permanent. Your account and associated data — profile, completed-session history, and settings tied to your account — will be deleted, except where retention is legally required. We’ll email you when deletion is complete. If you prefer, you can also delete your account in the app under Settings → Account → Delete Account.

Deleting your Allos account does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription. Store billing can continue until you cancel it in your store account — manage subscriptions with Apple or Google Play. Cancelling is not required to delete your account.

To verify it’s you, we’ll email a confirmation link to your account’s address. Nothing is deleted until you open that link.

Didn’t receive the email, or no longer have access to the address? Contact hello@allos.app and we’ll verify your identity and process the deletion for you.

Check Your Email

If an Allos account exists for , we’ve sent it a confirmation link. Open the link within 1 hour to confirm the deletion — nothing happens without it.

Don’t see it? Check your spam folder, or contact hello@allos.app for a support-assisted deletion.
