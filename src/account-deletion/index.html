Delete Your Allos Account

This page lets you request deletion of your Allos account without the app. Allos is published on Google Play and the App Store by Allos Development.

Deleting your account is permanent. Your account and associated data — profile, completed-session history, settings tied to your account, and your customer records with our subscription-management and email providers — will be deleted within 30 days of your request, once it is verified. The scope of what is deleted, and the few records retained with their retention periods, are described in our Privacy Policy.

The fastest way to delete your account is in the app, under Settings → Account → Delete Account. Guest profiles (using Allos without signing up) have no email address we can verify, so guest data is deleted in the app under Settings → Account → Delete Guest Data.

Deleting your Allos account does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription. Store billing continues until you cancel the subscription in your store account — manage subscriptions with Apple or Google Play. Cancelling is not required to delete your account.

To request deletion here, email hello@allos.app from the email address on your Allos account, with the subject “Account deletion request.” We’ll reply to acknowledge your request and verify that you control the address — nothing is deleted until you answer that reply, and a request with no answer after 14 days is closed without action. Once verified, we complete the deletion steps we control within the same 30 days counted from your original request, and we’ll email you a confirmation when they are done. Google Analytics may then take up to 63 days to permanently remove the analytics data we ask it to delete, as described in our Privacy Policy. If you no longer have access to the address on your account, use the in-app deletion flow above — it verifies your identity by signing in again, not by email.
